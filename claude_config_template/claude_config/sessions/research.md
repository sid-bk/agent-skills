# Session: Research

> Load alongside CLAUDE.md when evaluating tools, costs, vendors, or approaches.

---

## How to research here

- State the decision the research serves before gathering — don't boil the ocean.
- Cite sources for empirical claims; flag confidence when uncertain.
- For tool/vendor comparisons, use a table: option × {cost, latency, limits, lock-in, fit}.
- Distinguish what's measured from what's claimed by the vendor.
- End with a recommendation **and** the condition that would change it.

## Output

When a finding is confirmed, record it:
- Tool/vendor evaluated → `memory/stack.md` (role, version, cost, latency, verdict).
- Reusable conclusion → `memory/decisions.md` if it settles a decision.
- Note the date — pricing and limits drift.
